How do I cancel my Flavorly Plus subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple, not inside the app. On your iPhone open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, choose Flavorly and tap Cancel. If you cancel during the free trial before it ends, you will not be charged.

I reinstalled the app or got a new phone. Where is my subscription?

Sign in to Flavorly with the same account you subscribed with, then open the paywall and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure the phone is signed in to the same Apple ID that made the purchase.

What do I get for free?

You can browse the whole recipe library, filter by diet and allergens, and cook one recipe per rolling 7-day week with up to five favorites. Flavorly Plus removes the limits and adds the shopping list and offline saving.

How does allergen filtering work? Can I rely on it?

Declare your allergens in Settings and every recipe containing them (or any recipe we cannot confidently classify) is hidden from you everywhere in the app. It is deliberately cautious, but it is an aid, not a guarantee: ingredient brands vary, so if you have a serious allergy always double-check the actual products you cook with.

Which languages are supported?

Flavorly and all its recipes are available in English, Spanish, Portuguese (Brazil), French, Italian, German, Japanese, Korean, Simplified Chinese and Traditional Chinese. The app follows your phone's language, and you can pick a different one in Settings.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, go to Settings and tap Delete account. This permanently removes your account and data from our servers. Note that deleting the account does not cancel an active subscription, so cancel it in your Apple ID settings too. You can also email us and we will delete it for you.

How do refunds work?

Purchases are processed by Apple, so refunds go through them: visit reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in with your Apple ID and select the Flavorly purchase. Apple handles the request directly.

I found an error in a recipe.

Email us the recipe name, the step or ingredient, and what looks wrong. We verify against the source cookbook and fix it in a content update. No app update needed on your side.
