Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 8, 2026

Flavorly is a recipe app. To save your favourites, remember your dietary preferences and keep your shopping list across devices, the app uses an account and stores a small amount of data on our servers. This page explains exactly what that is, why we keep it, and how to delete it.

We do not show ads, we do not use advertising identifiers, and we do not sell or share your personal data.

Your account

You can sign in with your email address or with Sign in with Apple. We store:

  • Your email address (or, with Sign in with Apple, the identifier Apple provides — this may be a private relay address if you chose to hide your email).
  • A unique account ID.

We use this to sign you in and to keep your data tied to you. We do not send marketing email.

What we store with your account

  • Favourites — the recipes you have saved.
  • Dietary preferences — for example vegetarian or vegan.
  • Allergens — the ingredients you have told us to avoid.
  • Shopping list and fridge items you add in the app.
  • Cooking settings — language, units and similar preferences.

We use this data for one purpose: to run the features you use. Allergen and dietary information is used only to filter recipes and never for anything else.

A note on allergen information

Allergens you declare are health-related. We treat them as sensitive: they are stored only to filter what the app shows you, they are never used for advertising or profiling, they are never shared with third parties, and they are deleted with your account. You can change or remove them at any time in Settings.

Purchases and subscriptions

Flavorly Plus is a subscription processed by Apple's App Store. We never see or store your payment details.

To know which features to unlock, we use RevenueCat, a subscription-management service. RevenueCat receives your account ID and purchase receipts from Apple, and nothing else about you. Subscriptions are managed and cancelled through your Apple ID settings, not through the app.

Where your data is stored

Your data is stored with Supabase, our hosting provider, in the European Union (Ireland). It is protected in transit and at rest.

Images and content

Recipe photos and step images are delivered from our servers when you open a recipe. Those requests contain the standard technical information any internet request contains, such as your IP address, and are used only to deliver the content.

What we do not do

  • No advertising and no ad identifiers.
  • No third-party analytics or tracking across apps or websites.
  • No selling or sharing of personal data.
  • No use of your allergen or dietary information for anything other than filtering recipes.

Deleting your account and data

You can delete your account at any time from Settings → Delete account in the app. This permanently removes your account and everything listed above from our servers. If you signed in with Apple, we also revoke that sign-in with Apple.

Deleting your account does not cancel an active subscription — that is managed by Apple. Cancel it in your Apple ID settings before or after deleting the account.

You can also email us and we will delete your account for you.

Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of your data, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, by emailing the address below. If you are in the European Union or United Kingdom, you also have the right to lodge a complaint with your data protection authority.

Children

Flavorly is not directed at children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
